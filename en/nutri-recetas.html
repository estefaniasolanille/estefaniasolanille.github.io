Nutri Recetas

A landing page for a cooking brand invented from scratch, as a course assignment. The goal: get people to leave their email in exchange for 10 free recipes from the book Comida Real.

Decisions

  • Conversion: the "¡Quiero mi recetario!" ("I want my recipe book!") button appears on the home page and again on the final form, which only asks for name, email and diet type.
  • Identity: a 3-color palette (2 main and 1 accent) to mark the hierarchy between sections and highlight the calls to action.
  • Typography: sans-serif for body text and serif for headings and buttons.
